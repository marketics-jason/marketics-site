The principle

Commission what scales.
Fix-fee what's operational.

Revenue Earns a commission — because it scales with performance, and a commission keeps our incentive identical to yours. This is the part that should be paid for results.
Operations The running of the house — a fixed fee, per month or per reservation. It's real work, but it doesn't scale your revenue, so it shouldn't take a cut of it.
The inversion Most managers charge a revenue-style commission for operational work. We think that's backwards — and it's the reason a generalist's 20–30% quietly costs you far more than a specialist's 10%.

How the money moves
Per booking The 90/10 split is calculated on each reservation — not tallied and invoiced at month-end.
At payout Airbnb releases the payout about 24 hours after the guest checks in. The split happens there, at the source.
Separate accounts Your 90% and our 10% land in each of our own accounts. We never hold, touch, or route your money.
Nothing to pay No invoice, no chasing, no retainer. You can't fall behind on a fee you never have to send.
