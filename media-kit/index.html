Marketics is a performance-based short-term rental revenue firm that optimizes listings across pricing, distribution, ranking, and storytelling, and is paid on the lift it creates.

Long. 1 paragraph

Founded by Jason Baxter, Marketics is a full-stack short-term rental revenue specialist working with a select set of owners across 22 markets in the United States, Canada, and Mexico. Rather than manage the property, Marketics runs its market (market positioning, storytelling, distribution, ranking, and pricing) with a documented 45% median revenue lift, net of market movement. The firm operates on a performance model: it is paid a share of the incremental revenue it generates, aligning its fee with the owner's outcome.

Jason Baxter Founder · Marketics

Jason Baxter is the founder of Marketics. He spent five years operating a 50+ listing short-term rental portfolio of his own before building the specialist practice that now runs revenue for a select group of owners. A 35-time Airbnb Superhost, his work spans 22 markets across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, and includes 1 property accepted into the Airbnb Luxe collection.

Twenty years across technology, real estate, and luxury markets shaped his central conviction: a property earns more when the market can finally see what it's worth. He works and writes from Montréal.
