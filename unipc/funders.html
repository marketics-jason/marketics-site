The structural answer — and what already operates.

Seeks

UNIPC is being built as the structural answer: an organization governed by Indigenous nations that can receive at institutional standard and deliver at community truth — accreditation-grade fiduciary systems under construction, with regulated financial services pursued through proper authorization, announced only when licensed, never before.

Does · Today

A governing council across five continents; assembly governance since 2020; a treaty process engaging nations in every region; and a standing rule that would govern your money too — contributions are accepted unearmarked or not at all, and no single funder may dominate our budget. We were designed to be impossible to capture, including by you. We suspect that is why you would trust us.
