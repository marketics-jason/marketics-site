Principle

You are not joining someone else’s table.

IS

UNIPC was founded by Indigenous nations and peoples — over sixty co-founders, many regions, one assembly. The ones who built it faced what you face: papers that don’t name your nation, banks that don’t know your address, borders drawn through your homeland, and rooms where decisions about you are made without you.

What this is, plainly.

Does · Today

Today, UNIPC is where nations gather. We hold assemblies. We are writing a treaty of friendship between Indigenous nations — the kind our ancestors made long before modern states existed. We are building a registry to hold Indigenous treaties safely: yours, your grandparents’, and the ones your nation will make next.

Seeks · Tomorrow

Tomorrow, we intend more. Identity and travel documents that carry your nation’s name — issued only after the states involved agree to honor them, because a document that strands you at a border is worse than none. Financial services under real regulation — because your nation’s money deserves the same protection as any government’s. These are programs in design. We will tell you they exist only when they truly do. That is a promise.

What membership means.

Principle

Membership costs you nothing that is yours:

  • Your sovereignty stays yours. Joining takes no position on your nation’s status and waives no claim.
  • Your voice stays yours. Where your nation and UNIPC are both in a room, your nation speaks first.
  • Your treaties stay yours. The depositary holds them; it never owns them.
  • Your say is real. Nations — only nations — hold the vote.
Is

And if your nation lives inside a state that denies you exist: we have a protected path. Quiet accession. Names shielded. We were built by people who know why that matters.
