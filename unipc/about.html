The principle that outranks every other line

We seek each nation’s membership so it may be part of a growing collective voice — never so that we may speak for it.

Our charter binds us to it: the organization speaks only where the Assembly has expressly mandated, and wherever a member nation’s own voice is present, it precedes ours.

IS

We stand in a long line. Indigenous nations were making treaties across this world before most modern states existed, and Indigenous diplomats have kept an unbroken presence in the world’s halls — from the first petitions in Geneva a century ago to the delegations that return there still, season after season. We did not begin this work. We are building it a permanent home.
