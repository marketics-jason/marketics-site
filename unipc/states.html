For states & ministries

Precision is our protocol.

IS

UNIPC is a council of Indigenous nations and peoples, constituted in 2020, developing the instruments of a permanent Indigenous diplomacy. We are aware of what we are and candid about what we are not yet — and we submit that this candor is the strongest credential an emerging institution can present.

The category, correctly stated

No overclaim appears on this page. That is the point.

Is

We do not claim intergovernmental status; under the instruments that define the term, that status follows from state action, not self-declaration.

Seeks

Our road is the functional one, and it is precedented: entities from the ICRC to the Sovereign Military Order of Malta acquired international standing through exercised mandates, host-state arrangements, and accumulated recognition — not through a founding claim. We are walking that road in sequence: functions first, instruments second, status as the record’s conclusion.

What engagement offers a ministry.

Does

A single, organized counterpart for Indigenous diplomacy: a council spanning five continents, a treaty process in motion, a depositary function under construction, and protocols designed so that states and nations meet without prejudice to any position on status or territory.

Principle

Nothing in our instruments may be construed against the territorial integrity or political unity of states — that language is native to our charter, not appended for comfort.

Seeks · In order

What we will ask of interested states, in order — no omnibus asks, no surprises:

  1. 01
    Participation in convenings.
  2. 02
    Treaty conversation.
  3. 03
    In time, acceptance of member documentation — presented ministry by ministry, each instrument standing on its own review.
