PRIN.

The most useful thing an ally ever does is make room — amplify the voices of nations without ever replacing them. If that sentence sounds like relief rather than restriction, you belong here.

Is

UNIPC holds a place for allies and advocates: a defined membership tier with real work and honest boundaries.

Does

Allies extend our reach — translation, research, logistics, introductions, and the patient work of correcting the record wherever Indigenous institutions are misdescribed.

Principle

Allies do not vote, do not speak for nations, and do not set direction. What they do is make the road shorter. That has always been enough, and it has always mattered.
