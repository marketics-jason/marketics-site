Media room & brand use

Describe us accurately. It is the whole ask.

Everything a journalist, partner, or member needs to represent UNIPC correctly — the mark, the palette, the type, and the one rule that governs how we are named. Use it freely, within the register below.

How to refer to us.

Say this
  • ✓UNIPC — an organization of Indigenous nations and peoples.
  • ✓A council constituted by Indigenous nations, founded in 2020.
  • ✓Developing the instruments of a permanent Indigenous diplomacy.
Not this
  • ×Not an “intergovernmental organization” — that status follows from state action, not self-description.
  • ×Not a government, a state, or a representative of any single nation.
  • ×Not an NGO or charity in the ordinary sense — it is a council of nations.

The tense signal

Every claim is written in one of three tenses and marked so readers can tell fact from ambition.

ISWhat we are — present, verifiable.
DOESWhat we do — function, in operation.
SEEKSWhat we seek — ambition, never implied as fact.
