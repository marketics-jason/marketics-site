The thirty-second institution

What we are, what we do, and what we seek — each in its own tense, never blended.

IS — What we are

An organization of Indigenous nations and peoples, governed by its members, organized under a council structure spanning five continents — from the Arctic to Oceania, from the Sahel to the Andes.

DOES — What we do

We convene. Our General Assembly has met and resolved since 2020. We draft — a Multilateral Treaty of Friendship, Cooperation and Recognition, written by and for Indigenous nations, is in development with participants across every region. We build — the institutions of a permanent Indigenous diplomacy: a treaty depositary, standards for consent and documentation, and the protocols by which nations and states meet with respect.

SEEKS — What we seek
Not as gifts to be granted — as facts to be acknowledged.
  1. 01
    Standing.
  2. 02
    A headquarters agreement with a host state.
  3. 03
    Recognition by treaty-participant states across three continents.
  4. 04
    Regulated financial services worthy of our members.
  5. 05
    Travel documents that borders honor.

The road is mapped, the sequence is public, and we are walking it in the open.
