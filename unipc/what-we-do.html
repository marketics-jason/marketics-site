What we do

The mandate, in working order.

Does

We convene.

Assemblies of nations; forums where nations and states meet as counterparts.

Does

We keep the treaties.

The flagship function

The depositary in development will hold Indigenous treaties — historical instruments entrusted to safekeeping, new treaties between nations, and every agreement our members choose to lodge — authenticated, numbered, and preserved beyond any single state’s reach.

Does

We set standards.

Protocols for free, prior and informed consent; for data that belongs to the peoples it describes; for how documentation should name a nation.
