How the 10% works

If your property generates $5,000/month in net guest payouts, Marketics earns $500. If we lift that to $7,500/month, we earn $750. You keep 90% of everything — always.

Net payout = total guest payment minus Airbnb service fees and cleaning fees. The 10% is calculated on that net figure — never on the gross booking.

Example: A 2-bedroom condo in Montréal went from $1,700/month to $4,450/month in net payouts within 31 days of optimization — September to October 2025. 1 bedroom on St-Laurent in Le Plateau. Same property. Same location. Different positioning.

Common Questions

What hosts ask before starting.

How much will I pay?

Marketics charges 10% of your net payout per booking. No monthly retainer. No long-term contract. Engagements start with a $500 commitment deposit — refunded automatically when you complete the 90-day program.

What's included in the optimization?

Revenue management and dynamic pricing strategy, listing optimization (copy, photography direction, cover photo), improved search ranking on Airbnb, faster guest review collection, minimum stay optimization, and market demand intelligence. Guest messaging and day-to-day operations remain with the host or their property manager.

What happens if my revenue doesn't improve?

The deposit doesn't ride on results. A $500 commitment deposit is charged at onboarding and refunded automatically when you complete the 90-day program — the only condition is completion. Withdrawing before Day 90 forfeits the deposit. Our compensation is what rides on results: at 10% of bookings, we only earn more when you earn more.

What does "net payout" mean?

Net payout is the amount Airbnb deposits to you after deducting their service fees and any cleaning fees. It's not the gross booking amount. Marketics' 10% is calculated on this net figure.

How long before I see results?

Most properties see meaningful improvement within the first 30–60 days. Full optimization results, including faster guest review collection and search ranking gains, typically compound over 60–90 days.

Is there independent data on short-term rental market performance?

Yes. PriceLabs market data tracks occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR by city across North America — it's the same data infrastructure Marketics uses to manage pricing across our portfolio. For regulatory context, short-term rental regulations vary by market — Marketics operates only in markets where STR is permitted.
